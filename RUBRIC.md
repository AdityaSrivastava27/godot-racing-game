# AI Opponents Rubric

- 6 opponent cars start on the grid with the player
- Opponents don't move during the countdown and start at GO
- Opponents always drive forward, never in reverse
- Opponents go around the track in the same direction as the player
- Opponents stay on the road through every corner
- Opponents use the whole road, not only the edges
- Opponents don't scrape along the barriers
- Opponents drive around a slow or stopped car instead of stopping behind it
- Opponents recover and rejoin the race after being bumped or spun
- Opponents don't pass through each other or the player
- Wheels don't clip into the ground or float above it
- Player controls and the track are unchanged
- No errors in the log during a race
